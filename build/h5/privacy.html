Gobble Pop! — Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Last Updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: ynot-labs

Welcome to Gobble Pop! ("the Game"), developed and published by ynot-labs ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Gobble Pop! across Web portals (including CrazyGames, YouTube Playables, and GitHub Pages) as well as iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) devices.

1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Game Progress & Cloud Save Data: We store your level progress, star ratings, coins, skin shards, unlocked hole skins, and audio/haptic preferences locally on your device (via browser localStorage/IndexedDB or mobile local storage) and, where supported, synchronize your save state via your host platform's official Cloud Save API (e.g., CrazyGames SDK Data API, YouTube Playables Save API, Apple iCloud/Game Center, or Google Play Games).
  • Anonymous Gameplay Analytics (Opt-In / Consent-Based): With your consent via our in-game Privacy & Consent dialog, we log anonymous gameplay events (such as level start, level win/fail rates, and booster usage) to balance game difficulty and diagnose technical issues. No personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, phone number, or physical address is collected by the Game.
  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: When playing ad-supported versions on mobile or web portals, third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, or CrazyGames Ads) may process device identifiers (e.g., IDFA on iOS subject to ATT permission, AAID on Android, or browser cookies), coarse IP-based region, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant advertisements and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your gameplay progress across sessions and devices;
  • To improve level design, frame rate stability, and user experience;
  • To provide optional Rewarded Video bonuses and ad-supported free gameplay, as well as to process "Remove Forced Ads" in-app purchases and purchase restoration.

3. Advertising, Consent & Your Choices (GDPR / CCPA / ATT)

  • In-Game Privacy Controls: You can review or change your Analytics and Personalized Ads preferences at any time inside the Game by opening Settings (Gear Icon) → Privacy & Terms.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5+, we request your explicit permission via the system ATT prompt before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). If you decline, only contextual (non-personalized) ads will be shown.
  • Data Deletion: You can reset and erase all local game save data at any time via your browser/device settings or by appending ?reset=1 on web builds.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Gobble Pop! is a general-audience casual puzzle game rated 4+ / Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe personal data has been inadvertently provided, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Third-Party Platform Policies

Depending on where you play Gobble Pop!, the platform provider or integrated SDKs govern their own data processing under their respective privacy policies:

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • YouTube / Google Privacy Policy & AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy
